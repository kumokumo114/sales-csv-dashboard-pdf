A QUICK GUIDE

Your CSV, ready to go.

Use a UTF-8 CSV with commas or semicolons. Column names are case-insensitive; spaces and hyphens become underscores.

Column What to include
date YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY/MM/DD or US MM/DD/YYYY
product A non-empty product name
quantity A positive whole number (up to 1,000,000)
unit_price A non-negative number, up to two decimal places

Optional: order_id, region, category, currency. Without an order ID, each retained row counts as one order. Missing region/category values receive a default label.

Currency: USD (default), EUR, GBP, CAD or AUD. Use one currency per file; don't include currency symbols in prices. No exchange conversion is performed.

Cleaning: Exact duplicate normalized rows are removed. Invalid rows are excluded with a reason. Identical legitimate sales without unique order IDs may be treated as duplicates.

Your PDF: Two pages covering metrics, monthly trends, top products, active filters and the cleaning summary. Revenue is quantity × unit price; refunds, discounts and taxes are not modeled.
